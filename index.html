Al-Khwarizmi

Historical records show that scholars like Al-Khwarizmi shaped medieval science during the Islamic Golden Age. Working at the House of Wisdom in Baghdad, he laid foundational concepts for algebra and helped spread positional numerals across the world.

  • Full Name: Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi
  • Lifespan: c. 780 – c. 850 CE
  • Field: Mathematics, Astronomy, Geography
  • Famous Work: Al-Kitab al-Mukhtasar fi Hisab al-Jabr wal-Muqabala

His methods laid groundwork for modern computing, and the word Algorithm comes from his name.
